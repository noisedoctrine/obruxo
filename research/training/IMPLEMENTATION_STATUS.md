Current task: a broader combined control curriculum before data scaling, guided by the [completed recipe matrix](COMBINED_COVERAGE.md). Only 214 of 775 scalars vary under supervision across the full training pool; the sampler is disabled, each oscillator has one wavetable choice, and only two effects are enabled. The nested-state model has outputs for all six structured families, renders original LFO/map/custom-curve sources and separately measures representation and prediction errors. Its custom-curve curriculum covers three spectral modes in each oscillator and all three seed slots; its ordered connection curriculum remains limited. The expression extension adds eight performances, four MIDI-driven connections and a sampler that covers every reference performance in each sweep. Native transport, repeatability and feature ingestion pass; this is not evidence of learned performance invariance. The native seed-bit profile preserves signed/unsigned bit equivalence while silent audio remains rejected. This does not establish generality over arbitrary graphs or raw preset layouts. These patch capabilities remain necessary regardless of scores on the small development panel. Measured data scaling and combined training follow. Broad headroom/robustness, strict tiny-overfit and model quality remain open gates. Detailed weight tuning and repeated seed studies stay deferred. Final-test and release gates remain open.
