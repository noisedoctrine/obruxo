# Training-plan completion ledger

This ledger tracks the [training and release plan](../modelling/TRAINING_PLAN.md). An implemented experiment does not complete a quality gate. PRs are submitted for review and are never merged automatically. Compute remains free-first, audio is generated on demand, and GitHub Actions remains disabled.

Current task: custom warps/seeds, then broader combined coverage. The ordered connection stage extends the eight-curve model, uses original LFO and mapping audio, and separately measures representation and prediction errors. All 64 positions and the complete source/destination vocabulary have outputs; the authored training curriculum initially uses five patterns with up to sixteen connections. This does not establish learned generality over arbitrary graphs. These patch capabilities remain necessary regardless of scores on the small development panel. Broader performance coverage, measured data scaling and combined training follow. Broad headroom/robustness, strict tiny-overfit and model quality remain open gates. Detailed weight tuning and repeated seed studies stay deferred. Final-test and release gates remain open.
