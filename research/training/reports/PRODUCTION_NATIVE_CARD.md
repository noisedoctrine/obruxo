# Check production choices against real Vital

Declared 2026-10-23 before new native requests. The [software check](PRODUCTION_CHOICE_RESULTS.md) proves exact production choices and usable category derivatives. This experiment checks whether their local sound-loss direction helps actual Vital audio and whether that loss reaches the accepted predictor's weights. It performs **zero optimizer updates**, admits no new model, and keeps **14 dense outputs / 355,806 trainable parameters**.

Select 12 training patches, two from each unison/filter category. Use each patch twice: infer from its original recording and score against a wider performance, then infer from that wider recording and score against the original performance. Within each category, one patch uses a held wider note and one an alternate pattern. The two input groups therefore contain 12 cases each. Use the independently audited cached inputs and accepted earlier initializer; validate the full production-choice proof/cache before selection. No development or test state is used. Source settings and evaluator MIDI do not enter the predictor.

At each actual prediction, render raw production argmax with the qualified 32,768-frame lead and all oscillator/sampler random phases fixed. Render the source twice and require exact repeat and exact agreement with the audited recording. Measure bounded native continuous differences and every categorical alternative with the existing qualified estimator. Backpropagate the measured sound-only objective through the new adapter and actual predictor. Retain complete model-gradient digests and category/output gradients; model tensors and Torch RNG must remain unchanged.

Evaluate one fixed continuous step and one joint step. The continuous radius is **0.01 normalized units**, with its gradient divided by its largest absolute entry. This is a local check near actual model predictions, rather than the earlier deliberately perturbed starts with radius 0.05. For each category, move one fixed-scale logit unit along the normalized negative gradient, then use ordinary argmax. Backward temperature stays 1.0. There is no backtracking, best-of selection, parameter-label loss or hyperparameter search. A passing local output-space step is not evidence of a trained-model improvement.

Keep the earlier direction gates: separately within original-input and wider-input cases, both continuous and joint steps must improve MRSTFT by more than `1e-7` on at least **60%**, with strictly negative mean changes in both MRSTFT and the spectral/envelope/side objective. Every planned case, source repeat and accepted native result is mandatory. Sound loss must reach model weights; when a head's native alternatives differ by more than `1e-7`, both its output and head-weight gradients must be nonzero. Missing cases, changed weights/RNG, rejected audio or resource overruns prevent qualification.

Budget **48 source/repeat requests, at most 528 gradient requests and 48 changed-sound requests: 624 total native attempts**, with no automatic retries. Cap native execution at **45 minutes**, the native process tree at **8 GiB**, and evidence at **512 MiB**. Save measured source audio plus starting/continuous/joint audio for every case: 96 retained waveforms permit independent sound rescoring. This explicitly larger storage allocation replaces the earlier screen's scalar-only 32-MiB archive for this new experiment only. Calculate a conservative bound including four waveforms, two atomic-save copies and a ledger reserve before rendering; enforce the actual archive cap during execution. Inputs remain cached features; this is not a pre-rendered training-audio bank.

Preparation and execution-time input validation each have their own **10-minute** allowance and reported memory/time. The fresh offline auditor has **10 minutes / 4 GiB** and adds no native request or optimizer update. It reconstructs accepted inputs, production predictions, every request, native cost table, complete model-gradient digest, fixed step and gate. It hashes all source waveforms and recalculates all **72 starting/changed sound scores** from retained audio. Intermediate gradient-probe waveforms are not retained or independently rescored. Keep all prior costs, failed requests and partial case evidence.

Freeze the new source and `plan.json` before `reports/scripts/run_production_native_screen.py --execute`. Refuse an attempted run/output overwrite. Save case evidence after each completed case and keep a durable native launch; no learned optimizer state is at risk on battery. A pass permits preparation of the paired original/wider training runner under a separate frozen allocation. Historical fits remain unchanged. A2 reconstruction, fresh confirmation, listening and release stay open; local only, Actions disabled, Colab deferred.
