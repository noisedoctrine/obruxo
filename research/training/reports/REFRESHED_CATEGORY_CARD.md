# Check category feedback for refreshed initial weights

Status: the [completed zero-render result](REFRESHED_CATEGORY_RESULTS.md) passes. The native sound fit remains a separate experiment under its own plan and gates.

Protocol prepared 2026-10-22 before the new mathematical check. Use the retained accepted initializer with 404 patches and 16,000 updates, tensor hash `35cd356e97b5574df5d1a3cfaf19e79bd5b86f53df5e9ee6f56573a3da282c7b`. Neither the wider-only nor joint-replay model passed admission. Their verified source recordings remain usable independently of their rejected weights. Freeze the inference/checkpoint/cache hashes and machine-readable plan before execution. The historical category report is bound to other weights and cannot be relabelled.

Fit the same two fixed constants from 32 category-balanced **training-only** patches in the verified combined feature cache: per-head `max(1, RMS(scores - row mean))`, float64 accumulation. Use all four weighted records per patch: original, wider held note, original again, wider alternate. These are 128 weighted observations / 96 distinct recordings, matching the forthcoming sound-fit input proportions exactly. Category labels select representative states but do not enter the constant calculation. Require byte-identical fixed-phase production exports before/after scaling, unchanged continuous outputs and frozen model/frontend weights. In production use raw-score argmax.

Check the actual hard Gumbel sampler at temperatures 1.0 and 0.5 using 16 seeded draws for every input. Reconstruct its noise and RNG consumption exactly. For each head, compute the complete derivative matrix of its choice vector with respect to raw scores through the actual adapter and compare it with the independent softmax formula, relative tolerance 1e-5 / absolute tolerance 1e-8. This matrix determines the score gradient for **any** categorical native loss table: multiply its transpose by that table. A constant loss table should correctly give zero preference signal.

This is a mathematical connectivity check, not a new measurement of native sensitivity or sound quality. It does not require replaying an unrelated historical training trajectory or rendering new audio. The already qualified native estimator and renderer remain unchanged; the next matched sound fit must still report its actual native category gradients and pass unchanged sound-quality gates.

For each head and temperature require no exactly zero matrices, at least 99% of matrices with norm at least 1e-8 and median norm at least 1e-5. Rare large Gumbel draws can legitimately make a choice almost certain; a synthetic stress test rejected the initial 1e-6 cutoff for that tail before any refreshed-model query. Accumulate matrix norms in float64 so float32 squaring cannot falsely report an exact zero. If the original median norm is below 1e-5, require at least 10x median recovery; otherwise require at least half the original median, allowing already healthy scores to use scale one. A zero original median has no finite gain ratio; report it as null and require the same positive scaled median. Report all 4,096 sampled points, both heads and both temperatures, rather than selecting successful draws. No sweep or tuning extension.

Bind the new report to the accepted inference file, full training checkpoint and exact replay audit, complete input-cache file, pool/split manifest, all selected source/recipe/audio/feature identities, frontend digest, qualified renderer profile, Torch/Gumbel implementation and relevant source hashes. Validate both audited source banks, and check that the cache's original checkpoint is the retained initializer. The training loader recomputes constants and derivatives and rejects missing, changed, incomplete or incompatible evidence. Both matched training arms receive the same constants before their parameter or sound losses; raw production argmax remains unchanged.

Costs: **zero native requests, zero optimizer updates**, at most five minutes, 4 GiB process-tree RAM and 32 MiB new evidence, float32 CPU with two Torch threads. Execution includes input validation in its measured time/RAM. Freeze-only preparation also validates the inputs, but does not run the derivatives. A failed check stops admission of another full sound fit until diagnosed. Local only, no Colab or Actions changes. This does not advance A2 or qualify a release.
